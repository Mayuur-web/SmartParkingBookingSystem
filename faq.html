Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about booking a parking slot.
How do I book a parking slot? +
Sign up or log in, go to your Dashboard, filter slots by vehicle type or location, and click "Book Now" on any available slot. Fill in your vehicle number, date, start time and duration to confirm.
Can I cancel a booking? +
Yes. Go to "My Bookings," find the active booking you want to cancel, and click "Cancel." The slot will immediately become available again for others.
How is the parking cost calculated? +
Cost is calculated automatically as the selected duration (in hours) multiplied by the slot's hourly rate, shown to you before you confirm the booking.
Is there a mobile app? +
Not yet — the current version is a responsive website that works on both desktop and mobile browsers. A dedicated mobile app is on our roadmap.
How do I become an administrator? +
Administrator accounts are created by the facility owner. For this demo, use the admin login admin@parking.com / admin123 to explore the admin dashboard.
What payment methods are supported? +
This demo does not process real payments — cost is calculated and displayed for demonstration purposes. Payment gateway integration is listed under Future Scope.

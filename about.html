About Smart Parking
Why we built a simpler way to park.

Smart Parking Booking System started as a mini project with a simple goal: make it as easy to reserve a parking spot as it is to book a movie ticket. Searching for parking in busy areas wastes time, fuel, and patience — we wanted to fix that with a clean, web-based booking experience anyone can use from their phone or laptop.

Our platform gives drivers real-time visibility into slot availability across multiple locations, lets them reserve a spot in advance for a chosen date and duration, and keeps a full history of their bookings. On the other side, facility administrators get a single dashboard to manage slot inventory, track occupancy, and monitor revenue — no spreadsheets or paper registers required.

This project was developed as part of a Web Technology mini project curriculum, and is built entirely with HTML, CSS and JavaScript to demonstrate how far modern front-end web technology can go without any heavyweight backend infrastructure.
